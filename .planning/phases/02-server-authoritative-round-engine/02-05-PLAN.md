<objective>
Close the loop and prove it cannot hang.

The engine already writes, rates and ends rounds. This plan finishes the two remaining
transitions — a round with too few captions skipping straight past rating (D-09), and the
round-end beat that opens the next round or ends the game — then spends most of its effort on the
one property the whole phase exists for: **nothing in this engine ever waits for a person.**

Purpose: the party is a surprise on a fixed date with roughly a dozen phones in a loud room.
A round that ends slightly too early is a small failure; a round that hangs is the failure that
ends the evening. This plan is where that gets asserted rather than assumed.

Output: a game that plays itself from start to finish while players leave, lock their phones and
ignore their turn.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| server timer -> room state | The only thing that advances a round; this plan's whole job is to prove no other input can, and that none is required |
| player presence -> phase advancement | A player's connection state may only ever make a phase end sooner, never later — the asymmetry is what makes a hostile or absent player harmless |
| `Room` -> per-player snapshot | The round-end view reveals every caption and its ratings, which is correct only because it is built exclusively for `ROUND_END` and `GAME_END`, after every step has closed |
| room lifetime -> scheduled timers | A room that is disposed must leave nothing scheduled that could mutate it afterwards |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-02-09 | Denial of Service | a player holding a phase open by leaving, disconnecting or going silent | high | mitigate | Every non-terminal phase carries its own `setTimeout`; the phase-walk loop in `neverStalls.integration.test.ts` removes every player from each phase in turn and asserts it still advances |
| T-02-10 | Denial of Service | a phase timer firing against a disposed room | medium | mitigate | `clearPhaseTimer()` inside `dispose()`, asserted from each of the four non-terminal phases |
| T-02-19 | Denial of Service | a round stalling on a single-caption rating step nobody may rate | high | mitigate | `closeWriting` compares the rotation length against `MIN_SUBMISSIONS_TO_RATE` and skips the rating phase entirely below 2, so a step whose only eligible rater is its own barred author can never be entered |
