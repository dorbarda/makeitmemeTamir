<objective>
Open the writing phase for real: players type a caption and send it, everyone watches the count
climb, and the phase ends on the server's clock whether or not the last person ever pressed send.

Two rules make this phase what it is. The server closes the phase at its deadline regardless of
who has submitted (ROUND-04), and it may only ever pull that deadline *earlier* — never later
(D-07). And no player's caption text reaches another player's device before the round closes
(D-14) — enforced by leaving it out of the snapshot entirely, not by hiding it in the UI.

Purpose: this is the half of the round where the room is most likely to freeze waiting for one
person. Closing it on a clock nobody can influence is the whole point of the phase.

Output: a writing phase that fills up, shows friendly social pressure, and always ends.
</objective>

|----------|-------------|
| client socket -> `submit-caption` handler | The caption text is fully attacker-controlled, unbounded in length and may contain control characters; the author identity is taken from `socket.data`, never from the payload |
| `Room` -> per-player snapshot | `snapshotFor(playerId)` is the only place a player's view is built; a caption omitted here cannot leak to anyone, including someone reading raw socket frames |
| `Room` -> its own deadline | Only `collapseDeadline` may change a live deadline, and only in the earlier direction |
