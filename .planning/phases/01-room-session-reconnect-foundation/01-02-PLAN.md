<objective>
Make the door impossible to be blocked at. Names are free text (D-06), capped at 15 user-perceived
characters with emoji allowed (D-08), sanitized server-side, and de-duplicated by auto-numbering
rather than rejection (D-07). A player can fix their name while the room is still in the lobby and
is locked out of renaming once play begins (D-09). The room holds at most 20 players and the 21st
gets a clear Hebrew message instead of a broken screen (D-05), and the room reports that it can
start only once 3 players are in (D-11).

Purpose: LOBBY-04's roadmap wording says duplicates are "prevented"; CONTEXT.md's D-07 supersedes it
with auto-numbering. Getting this backwards is the single most likely way this phase ships a flow
that turns a guest away at a party. LOBBY-04 is satisfied by distinguishability, not by rejection.

Output: a grapheme-correct name pipeline, an auto-numbering de-duplicator, a lobby-only rename path,
the capacity cap, and the shared Hebrew message map every later phase reuses.
</objective>

<threat_model>
## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| phone browser -> `join-room` / `create-room` / `rename` | Untrusted free-text display name crosses here and is then rendered on every other player's screen |
| phone browser -> join/create intents | Unbounded call rate from a single socket |

## STRIDE Threat Register

| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |
|-----------|----------|-----------|----------|-------------|-----------------|
| T-01-07 | Tampering | display name rendered into every roster | high | mitigate | `sanitizeName` strips Unicode Cc/Cf, zero-width and bidi-control characters server-side before storage. Without this, a name containing U+202E reverses the rendering of the rest of a roster row on every other player's phone — a real risk specifically because this app is RTL. React's JSX escaping covers markup injection; it does not cover bidi controls, which is why this is a server-side strip and not a rendering concern. |
| T-01-08 | Tampering | grapheme length cap | medium | mitigate | Length is capped by grapheme count, not UTF-16 length, so invisible or multi-code-point characters cannot be used to smuggle a name far longer than the visual cap past the check. |
| T-01-09 | Denial of Service | `join-room` / `create-room` handlers | medium | mitigate | `ROOM_CAPACITY` caps a room at 20 players and a per-socket sliding-window guard caps create/join intents at 10 per 10 seconds. The guard deliberately excludes `rejoin` so a flaky phone is never locked out. |
| T-01-10 | Spoofing | display name as identity | medium | mitigate | Names are never used as identity — auto-numbering keeps them distinguishable for humans, but every server-side lookup keys on `playerId`, so two players choosing the same name cannot be confused for each other by any code path. |
| T-01-11 | Denial of Service | `resolveDisplayName` suffix scan | low | accept | The first-free-number scan is bounded by the number of players in the room, which is capped at 20. No unbounded loop is reachable. |
</threat_model>
