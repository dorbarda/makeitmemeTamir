<objective>
Make a dropped phone a non-event. A player who locks their screen or walks into a WiFi dead zone
stays in the roster through a grace delay before fading (D-13), keeps their record and score
outright once play has begun (D-17), and comes back onto whatever screen the room is currently
showing with no popup and no extra tap (D-14). If the host's phone dies, host powers move to another
connected player after a longer grace and the original host returns as an ordinary player (D-16).
Nothing the player was typing survives the trip (D-15) — deliberately.

Purpose: iOS Safari drops backgrounded WebSocket connections, sometimes with no close event at all,
and this is the primary failure this phase exists to defeat. Socket.IO's connection-state recovery
reports false across a genuine network-interface change, so the explicit token rejoin plus a
foreground-triggered full resync is the mechanism, not a fallback.

Output: shared grace-delay constants, roster-fade and host-transfer behaviour under fake timers, the
three converging client resync triggers, and a Hebrew reconnecting indicator.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| phone browser -> `rejoin` handshake | Untrusted token crosses here and, if mishandled, becomes someone else's identity |
| phone browser -> `request-resync` | Unbounded call rate from a socket that may be reconnecting repeatedly |
| server timers -> room state | Delayed callbacks mutate room state after the triggering socket is long gone |
